Privacy Policy — Android

Effective date: August 14, 2026

S-Tier Eats ("the App") is a small, independent application that helps you discover restaurants in The Woodlands, Texas area, place them on a personal S/A/B/C/F tier list, and (optionally) contribute to a community consensus ranking. This policy describes exactly what the Android version of the App accesses, what it stores, and what it never does. (A separate policy covers the iOS version, which uses Apple iCloud instead of the Google services described here.)

Sign-in and identity

Browsing restaurants, the map, and the community rankings requires no account. To rank restaurants or contribute to the community boards, you sign in with Google Sign-In, handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). When you sign in:

  • The App receives a stable, opaque user identifier and your Google account display name, which it uses to attribute your rankings and (optionally) show a name you choose on your profile.
  • Firebase Authentication, operated by Google, manages the underlying account credential. The App itself does not store your Google password and does not use your email address for any purpose.

You can sign out at any time from the Profile tab.

Information stored in the cloud (Google Firestore)

The App uses Google Cloud Firestore to power its community features. The following data is stored, attributed to your opaque user identifier:

  • Tier placements: when you place a restaurant in S/A/B/C/F, that placement (restaurant + tier) is saved so it can be averaged into the community consensus and re-loaded on your other devices.
  • Dietary/needs tags: if you confirm a tag (e.g. "gluten-free," "vegetarian") for a restaurant, that confirmation is saved so it can be counted toward the community tally shown on that restaurant.
  • Visited list: restaurants you mark as visited, stored so the list syncs to your other devices.
  • "Permanently closed" reports: if you mark a restaurant as closed, that report is saved as a count toward the closed flag shown to other users.
  • Display name (optional): a name you enter on the Profile tab. In this version it is not publicly shown next to your rankings.

This data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Google's handling of Firestore and Firebase Authentication data is governed by Firebase's privacy and security information and Google's privacy policy.

How the data is used

  • Showing you and other users a community consensus tier list and per-restaurant rankings and dietary tallies
  • Re-syncing your own placements and visited list across your devices
  • Basic app functionality only

None of this data is used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics, and none of it is sold or shared with any third party for their own use.

Maps

The App displays a map using the Google Maps SDK for Android. When the map loads, Google receives standard request data (such as device IP address and basic device characteristics) to render the map tiles, governed by Google's privacy policy. This release does not request access to your device location — the map is centered on the coverage area, and there is no "near me" feature. If a location feature is added in a future version, this policy and the App's permissions will be updated first.

What the App does not do

  • Does not access your device location, contacts, photos, or files
  • Does not contain third-party analytics or ad-tracking SDKs, and does not perform cross-app tracking
  • Does not collect your email, phone number, or payment information
  • Does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • Does not display advertising in this release

External links

Restaurant detail pages may hand off to other apps you have installed (for example, a website or a maps/directions app). Tapping such a link sends only the restaurant's name or coordinates to the destination app, whose own privacy policy then applies.

Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See our account & data deletion page for details, or email the address below. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.
