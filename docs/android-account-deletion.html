Delete Your Account & Data

S-Tier Eats · Android · Effective August 14, 2026

This page explains how to delete your S-Tier Eats account and the data associated with it, as required by Google Play.

Option 1 — In the app

Open S-Tier Eats → Profile tab → Delete account. Confirm when prompted. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data (see the list below). This is the fastest method and requires no waiting.

Option 2 — By email request

Email acompofelice@outlook.com from any address and include the display name you used in the app (or state that you'd like all data for your account removed). Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days. No account login is required to make this request.

What gets deleted

All data attributed to your account identifier is permanently removed, including:

  • Your tier placements (S/A/B/C/F rankings)
  • Your dietary/needs tag confirmations
  • Your visited list
  • Any "permanently closed" reports you submitted
  • Your profile, including any display name you set
  • Your Firebase Authentication account record

What is not affected

Deleting your account removes your contributions. The shared restaurant catalog and the aggregate community rankings remain (your individual placements are removed from the aggregate). Deleting your account does not delete your underlying Google account, which is managed by Google.

Data retention

Once deleted, your data is not retained. Routine encrypted backups of the database may persist for a short period as part of standard Google Cloud infrastructure and age out automatically; deleted data is not restored from them.
